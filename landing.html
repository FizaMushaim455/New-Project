About the Platform

Built for the voiceless patient

In critical care wards, patients who are intubated, paralyzed, or in extreme distress are cut off from communication. SilentCare Pro restores that voice — using nothing but a standard webcam and the power of AI.

By tracking 478 facial landmarks and 21 hand joints in real-time, the system converts gestures, gaze, and distress signals into spoken words — instantly alerting nursing staff.

Core Capabilities

Four pillars of silent communication

Every feature is purpose-built for the clinical environment — delivering maximum expressiveness with zero physical ability required.

Gesture Recognition

ASL & Gesture-to-Speech

21-point hand landmark tracking translates American Sign Language and custom gestures into context-aware sentences via Google Gemini 1.5 Flash — spoken aloud in English or Urdu.

Gaze Interaction

Gaze-Dwell Interaction

Designed for quadriplegic patients with zero limb movement. By focusing on an on-screen icon for 2 seconds, patients can request Water, Food, Help, or Pain relief — no hands required.

Emotional AI

Emotional AI & Distress Monitoring

478 facial blendshapes monitor for pain, grimacing, and SOS blink patterns (3 rapid blinks). Critical distress auto-bypasses manual inputs, sending an immediate alert to the nursing station.

Nurse Command Center

Live Ward Command Center

A real-time room status map with urgency strobes, WebSocket-powered patient alerts, and a full patient communication log — giving nurses complete situational awareness across the entire ward.

System Architecture

From camera to care action

A 5-stage real-time pipeline converts raw webcam frames into meaningful clinical actions in under 100ms.

01

Data Acquisition

The system captures a live webcam feed at 60 frames per second from any standard hospital-grade camera — no special hardware required.

60 FPS · WebRTC
02

Feature Extraction

MediaPipe processes each frame to extract 478 facial landmarks and 21 hand joint coordinates, capturing micro-expressions and fine motor signals.

MediaPipe · On-device inference
03

AI Intent Classification

Landmark sequences are sent to Gemini 1.5 Flash, which interprets gesture patterns and returns a natural-language request string — politely phrased and clinically appropriate.

Google Gemini 1.5 Flash · LLM inference
04

Bilingual Voice Synthesis

The interpreted request is immediately spoken aloud in English or Urdu using the Web Speech API with natural regional accent tuning.

Web Speech API · EN & UR
05

Real-Time Nurse Alert

A WebSocket event is broadcast to the Nurse Command Center, triggering a color-coded urgency strobe on the ward live map with the patient's room and request details.

Socket.IO · <100ms latency
